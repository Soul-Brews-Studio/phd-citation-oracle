# Bulot (2023) — Reference-Grade from Low-Cost Sensors

**Key findings** — PMS5003 calibrated RMSE down to 3.0-3.1 ug/m3. Between-unit uncertainty ~1.1-1.2 ug/m3. Twice-yearly calibration recommended.

**Thesis relevance** — Same Plantower sensor family as DustBoy; demonstrates calibratable to reference-grade.

**Full citation** — Bulot, F. M. J., et al. (2023). Characterisation and calibration of low-cost PM sensors at high temporal resolution to reference-grade performance. *Heliyon*, 9(5), e15943.
