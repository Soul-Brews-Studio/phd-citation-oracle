# Cho et al. (2024) — First GEMS Aerosol Results

**Key findings** — AOD R=0.792 (raw), R=0.920 (post-correction). 52.93% met +/-0.1 uncertainty threshold.

**Thesis relevance** — Foundational GEMS accuracy paper. Raw GEMS underestimates high AOD, overestimates low AOD -- critical for burning season comparison.

**Full citation** — Cho, Y., Kim, J., Go, S., et al. (2024). First atmospheric aerosol-monitoring results from the Geostationary Environment Monitoring Spectrometer (GEMS) over Asia. *Atmospheric Measurement Techniques*, 17(14), 4369-4390.
