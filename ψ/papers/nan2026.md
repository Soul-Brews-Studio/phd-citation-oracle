# Nan et al. (2026) — Multi-Task Calibration Framework

**Key findings** — _to fill after reading_ — multi-task calibration framework applied to an integrated low-cost PM sensor. Read for the task-decomposition design and what it reports as its own error bounds.

**Thesis relevance** — Multi-task calibration is the nearest published relative of a per-sensor confidence score: both take one raw sensor stream and produce several outputs of differing reliability. Compare its framing of "which output do you trust when" against the 5-factor A–F grading, and check whether it reports agreement (LoA/CCC) or only correlation.
