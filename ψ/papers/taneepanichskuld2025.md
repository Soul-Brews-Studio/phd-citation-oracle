# Taneepanichskuld (2025) — PM2.5 and Mortality, Thailand

**Key findings** — Significant PM2.5-mortality correlations. 6% increase in deaths during high-pollution periods. Stronger in central and northern provinces.

**Thesis relevance** — Links satellite PM2.5 (one comparison source) with mortality, validating multi-source monitoring accuracy.
