# Nakapan & Hongthong (2022) — MAIAC Surface Reflectance PM2.5, N. Thailand

**Key findings** — R = 0.83 to 0.84 against AERONET. Wavelength: MAIAC 550 nm (interpolated operationally from the 470 nm retrieval) compared to AERONET 550 nm (interpolated via Ångström Exponent). PM10 spatial correlation reached 0.75 via Geographically Weighted Regression. Strictly limited by polar orbital dynamics. Overpasses completely miss the late-afternoon and early-evening accumulation phases characteristic of northern Thailand valley inversions.

**Thesis relevance** — Establishes MAIAC as the gold standard for high-spatial-resolution modeling in the exact region of interest, provided researchers are willing to sacrifice hourly diurnal validation for absolute spatial fidelity.

**Full citation** — Nakapan, S., Hongthong, A. (2022). Applying surface reflectance to investigate the spatial and temporal distribution of PM2.5 in Northern Thailand. *ScienceAsia*, 48, 1-8. DOI: 10.2306/scienceasia1513-1874.2022.001.
