# Ponsawansong et al. (2023) — Oxidative Potential, Chiang Mai

**Key findings** — Biomass burning = 57.9% of PM2.5 during haze. Secondary inorganic 26.2%, vehicles 7.8%.

**Thesis relevance** — Health-relevant context for why accurate PM2.5 monitoring during burning season is critical.
