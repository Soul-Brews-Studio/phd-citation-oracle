# Barkjohn et al. (2022) — Extreme Wildfire Smoke Correction

**Key findings** — 12 of 13 sensors met EPA targets post-correction. Nonlinear response at >300 ug/m3 addressed via quadratic fit.

**Thesis relevance** — DustBoy experiences PM2.5 up to 280 ug/m3 during burning season, right at the critical correction boundary.

**Full citation** — Barkjohn, K. K., Holder, A. L., Kettler, J. P., & Clements, A. L. (2022). Correction and accuracy of PurpleAir PM2.5 measurements for extreme wildfire smoke. *Sensors*, 22(24), 9669.
