# van Donkelaar & Hammer (2021) — WashU/ACAG SatPM2.5

**Key findings** — Spatial CV R2=0.92, temporal CV R2=0.85. Known bias in data-sparse tropical regions.

**Thesis relevance** — Most widely used satellite PM2.5 for health studies. DustBoy ground truth fills known tropical data gaps.
