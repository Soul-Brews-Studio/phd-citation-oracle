# Barkjohn et al. (2021) — US-wide PurpleAir Correction

**Key findings** — Raw PurpleAir overestimates PM2.5 by ~40%. Correction reduces RMSE from 8 to 3 ug/m3. Corrected sensors report accurate NowCast AQI 90% of the time (vs. 75% uncorrected).

**Thesis relevance** — Gold standard PurpleAir correction; DustBoy R2=0.909 outperforms most uncorrected PurpleAir values.

**Full citation** — Barkjohn, K. K., Gantt, B., & Clements, A. L. (2021). Development and application of a United States-wide correction for PM2.5 data collected with the PurpleAir sensor. *Atmospheric Measurement Techniques*, 14(6), 4617-4637.
