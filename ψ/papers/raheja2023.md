# Raheja et al. (2023) — Multi-Brand Sensor Intercomparison, Accra

**Key findings** — After GMR correction: PurpleAir R2=0.86, QuantAQ R2=0.87. XGBoost best within training range but failed extrapolation.

**Thesis relevance** — DustBoy R2=0.909 exceeds all post-correction values. Validates developing-country sensor networks in top-tier journals.
