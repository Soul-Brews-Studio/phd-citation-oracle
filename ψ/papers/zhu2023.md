# Zhu et al. (2023) — Review: Satellite PM2.5 Challenges

**Key findings** — 40-80% satellite AOD missing due to cloud/surface contamination. ML susceptible to error in data-sparse regions.

**Thesis relevance** — Documents why ground sensors like DustBoy are irreplaceable -- continuous data regardless of cloud cover.
