# Jang et al. (2025) — GEMS AOD Validation over Mainland SEA

**Key findings** — Overall R2=0.75. Dry season R2=0.63-0.79. Rainy season R2 drops to 0.11-0.13. Distinct April AOD peak.

**Thesis relevance** — DIRECTLY validates GEMS over DustBoy's geographic domain. Seasonal accuracy collapse (dry ~0.75 vs wet ~0.12) is critical for interpreting DustBoy-vs-GEMS comparison.

**Full citation** — Jang, B., Stratoulias, D., Aekakkararungroj, A., et al. (2025). Validation and analysis of GEMS aerosol optical depth product against AERONET over mainland Southeast Asia. *Aerosol and Air Quality Research*, 25, 22.
