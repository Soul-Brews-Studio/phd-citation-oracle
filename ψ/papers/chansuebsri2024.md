# Chansuebsri et al. (2024) — PM2.5 Source Apportionment, Chiang Mai

**Key findings** — Average PM2.5 of 116+/-35 ug/m3 during smoke-haze. Biomass burning contributed 59.3 ug/m3 (dominant). Main source area: Thai-Myanmar border.

**Thesis relevance** — Definitive source apportionment for Chiang Mai. The 8x burning-to-monsoon ratio in DustBoy data is consistent with these findings.

**Full citation** — Chansuebsri, S., Kolar, P., Kraisitnitikul, P., et al. (2024). Chemical composition and origins of PM2.5 in Chiang Mai (Thailand) by integrated source apportionment and potential source areas. *Atmospheric Environment*, 327, 120517.
