# Meng et al. (2015) — VIIRS AOT Variability over China

**Key findings** — R = 0.907, accuracy = -0.058, precision = 0.192. Wavelength evaluated at the M-band 550 nm pixel. Exhibits high uncertainty over rapidly changing surface albedos and requires aggressive QA filtering that drastically reduces spatial coverage during severe, multi-day haze episodes.

**Thesis relevance** — VIIRS provides excellent radiometric precision, but the lack of a dedicated, peer-reviewed validation over mainland Southeast Asia between 2021 and 2026 limits its immediate defensibility compared to MAIAC.

**Full citation** — Meng, F., Cao, C., Shao, X. (2015). Spatio-temporal variability of Suomi-NPP VIIRS-derived aerosol optical thickness over China in 2013. *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 163, 61-69. DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2015.03.005.
