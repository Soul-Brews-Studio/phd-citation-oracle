# Jaffe et al. (2023) — EPA Correction in Smoke/Dust Events

**Key findings** — Corrections accurate for smoke events but underestimate dust by 5-6x. High-concentration divergence above 300 ug/m3.

**Thesis relevance** — Thailand burning season creates smoke-dominated aerosol; validates correction approach for biomass burning context.

**Full citation** — Jaffe, D. A., Miller, C., Thompson, K., Finley, B., Nelson, M., Ouimette, J., & Andrews, E. (2023). An evaluation of the U.S. EPA's correction equation for PurpleAir sensor data in smoke, dust, and wintertime urban pollution events. *Atmospheric Measurement Techniques*, 16(5), 1311-1322.
