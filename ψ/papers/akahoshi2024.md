# Akahoshi et al. (2024) — Open Burning Control Barriers

**Key findings** — Social/institutional barriers cause 2x PM2.5 emissions over 10-20 years. Cost ~$14M/year.

**Thesis relevance** — Monitoring networks like DustBoy needed to verify whether burning policies work.
