# Marvin et al. (2024) — Biomass Burning Aerosol Uncertainty, SE Asia

**Key findings** — Monthly attributable deaths underestimated by up to 4,500 in March. Emission inventory uncertainties propagate to PM2.5 and health.

**Thesis relevance** — If satellite products rely on emission inventories for gap-filling, burning uncertainty directly affects their Thai accuracy. DustBoy provides independent verification.

**Full citation** — Marvin, M. R., Palmer, P. I., Yao, F., Latif, M. T., & Khan, M. F. (2024). Uncertainties from biomass burning aerosols in air quality models obscure public health impacts in Southeast Asia. *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics*, 24, 3699-3715.
