# Dejchanchaiwong et al. (2023) — Seasonal Calibration in Thailand

**Key findings** — PMS7003 at 3 Thai sites. Low PM + high humidity reduced performance. Northern Thailand performed better than Bangkok in dry season.

**Thesis relevance** — CLOSEST published work to DustBoy thesis. Same country, sensor family, seasonal context. DustBoy thesis extends from 3 sites to 834.

**Full citation** — Dejchanchaiwong, R., Tekasakul, P., Saejio, A., Limna, T., Le, T.-C., Tsai, C.-J., Lin, G.-Y., & Morris, J. (2023). Seasonal field calibration of low-cost PM2.5 sensors in different locations with different sources in Thailand. *Atmosphere*, 14(3), 496.
