# Saksakulkrai et al. (2026) — Molecular Markers, Northern Thailand

**Key findings** — Three distinct biomass burning sources: mixed (31%), local (19%), aged/transported (13%). Aged aerosols from mixed burning dominate episodic haze.

**Thesis relevance** — Aged vs. local aerosol distinction affects light-scattering sensor accuracy differently.

**Full citation** — Saksakulkrai, S., Chantara, S., et al. (2026). Unveiling the origins of Northern Thailand's haze: Comprehensive chemical characterization and source apportionment of PM2.5 using targeted molecular markers. *Journal of Environmental Sciences*, 159, 635-648.
