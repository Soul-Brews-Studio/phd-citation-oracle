# Barkjohn et al. (2025) — Long-Term Evaluation of Six Sensor Types

**Key findings** — 58 sensors, 6 models, 7 US sites, 18 months. PurpleAir R2=0.67-0.94. Common failures: zeros, false highs, baseline shift, RH influence.

**Thesis relevance** — ESSENTIAL benchmark for long-term performance. DustBoy's 2.6B records over years is longer. Exclusion rules directly address identified failure modes.

**Full citation** — Barkjohn, K. K., Yaga, R., Thomas, B., Schoppman, W., Docherty, K. S., & Clements, A. L. (2025). Evaluation of long-term performance of six PM2.5 sensor types. *Sensors*, 25(4), 1265.
