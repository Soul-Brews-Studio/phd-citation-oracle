# O et al. (2025) — GEMS AOD to Hourly PM2.5 via ML

**Key findings** — RF and XGBoost on GEMS AOD + meteorology for hourly PM2.5 in South Korea. Strong temporal/spatial capture.

**Thesis relevance** — Demonstrates pipeline from GEMS AOD to PM2.5 estimates; method adaptable for Northern Thailand.

**Full citation** — O, S., et al. (2025). Estimating hourly ground-level aerosols using Geostationary Environment Monitoring Spectrometer aerosol optical depth. *Atmospheric Measurement Techniques*, 18, 1471-1484.
