# Yu et al. (2023) — Synergistic Ground + Geostationary Satellite

**Key findings** — Geostationary hourly PM2.5 has mean bias of -21.4% (hourly), -15.3% (daily) vs ground observations.

**Thesis relevance** — BRIDGES THE GAP -- shows how ground sensors, satellites, and models combine; directly informs multi-source comparison framework.
