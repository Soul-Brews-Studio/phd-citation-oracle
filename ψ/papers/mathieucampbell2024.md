# Mathieu-Campbell et al. (2024) — PurpleAir under High RH

**Key findings** — SSC model R2=0.74, RMSE=2.94. 16-23% error reduction over Barkjohn. Nonlinear RH effect at 50% threshold.

**Thesis relevance** — Chiang Mai monsoon RH >80%; nonlinear PM2.5/RH relationship directly applicable to DustBoy seasonal behavior.

**Full citation** — Mathieu-Campbell, M. E., Guo, C., Grieshop, A. P., & Richmond-Bryant, J. (2024). Calibration of PurpleAir low-cost particulate matter sensors: model development for air quality under high relative humidity conditions. *Atmospheric Measurement Techniques*, 17(22), 6735-6749.
