# Jin et al. (2022) — CAMS and MERRA-2 Validation

**Key findings** — CAMS overestimates; MERRA-2 underestimates. Large spatially varying biases, especially in undermonitored regions.

**Thesis relevance** — Documents known biases in reanalysis products for multi-source comparison context.
